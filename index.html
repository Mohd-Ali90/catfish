Welcome to CatFish

Roll the die to move the cat 🐈 around the board.The rules are as follows:-

1- If you land on 🟥, your next roll will move you higher on the board

2- If you land on 🟦, your next roll will move you lower on the board

3- If you land on ⬛, you have fallen into a hole and the game is over. You may start a new game

4- If you land on 🐠, you have found a fish - Delicious. You may roll again, you never know.
